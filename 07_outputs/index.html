Week 7: Outputs

Document your minimum viable project either here or in the Final Project tab.

Create a microcontroller program that integrates an input and output device, using the C++ class structure and avoiding the delay() function. Use the oscilliscope to measure the signals on one of the pins of your output device.

I had a lot of problems with my MVP. I was planning to have a box such that there would be my embroidered cloth and 2 arms. Most of the process was me trying to learn and make the inkStitch for my cloth since I was able to quickly scan and 3D print my arms early. What I didn't expect, however, is the complexity of the embroidery machine. I roped in many people like Nathan and Hannah, and asked Victor and Jonathan and Diego if they knew how to use the machine—which they didn’t. At some point I spent so much time on the machine which ended up giving me nothing, I didn't have much time to do my MVP as a whole. In the end, I quickly got together a blank of 3 mm of plywood, the fabric, and set it up to see if the effect was correct. It was.
