Week 9: Networking

Working with a partner or group of 3, program something that makes use of the radio, Wifi, or bluetooth capabilities of the ESP32. Only one person per group needs to have documentation on their website. Everyone else should provide a link to that person's documentation.

For this group, I decided to partner with Matthew and Gurmani since I do not believe that my own project requires bluetooth. After some debate, we decided that we will make a sort of dragon with a fan in it's head with streamer-like things in its mouth, such that when it is turned on remotely, the fan will blow the streamers out of it's mouth like fire. Additionally, we could also make LED eyes, maybe glowing different colors with different intensities of fan-blowing. I was tasked with making the head in blender, which I was able to quickly do. Gurmani would handle the rest as she has a plan to use a tempelate online where the body is moveable. Matthew is tasked with the coding of the piece.

We continued with the plan above, and we were successful. We also planned to have matching T-Shirts, which was something that I was tasked with along with making the head of the dragon. While I wasn't able to make that in time, we got the main stuff done.

- PROBLEM: We can't fit the fan inside of the dragon head like we thought we could. SOLUTION: We used a sort of wind-pipe to guide the airflow to make it seem like it was blowing air

- PROBLEM: Couldn't drill such that there could be 2 LED for the eyes SOLUTION: We put an eyepatch and made Spike cooler.

Our team was so passionate about Project Spike that we made a T-Shirt about it.
